Instructions

  1. (In Self Placement Mode) Each ship in the staging area represents one of your ships. Drag a ship onto your board for placement.
  2. (In Self Placement Mode) Use the "Change Ship Direction" button to toggle ship between vertical and horizontal before placing it.
  3. Ships can't touch. Ships must have at least one empty cell of space between them and any other ship — plan your placement accordingly.
  4. Once all ships are placed, click (or tap) a cell on the Computer's board to attack at that location.
  5. A red cell marked with a symbol means a hit. A gray cell marked with an "x" means a miss. Black cells represents un-attacked ships.
  6. After your attack, the computer automatically takes its turn, attacking at your board. Turns keep alternating until the game ends.
  7. Watch out! Once the computer scores a hit on one of your ships, it will target nearby cells to try to sink the rest of that ship.
  8. The first player to sink all of the opponent's ships wins.
  9. Play again. Use the "Reset Game" button to start a new game at any time, or "Switch Mode" to try the other placement style.
